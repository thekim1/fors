# Knowledge load test: eneo vs. the RAG platform + gateway

60 staff chatting back to back (no time to read), 10 s ramp, then 60 s measured; 30 % follow-ups in the same conversation. Requests started in the hold are measured to the end of their stream. Fake LLM as glm-5.3-flash (see README.md); the platform under test on 4 vCPUs and 18 GB.

## What staff experience

| Platform | Requests | Answers/min | Errors | First token p50 | p95 | p99 | Gap p50 | Gap p99 | Longest stall p95 | Whole answer p50 | p95 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| eneo v2.3.1 | 120 | 120 | 0 | 2.77 s | 11.51 s | 12.19 s | 25 ms | 66 ms | 682 ms | 24.98 s | 33.97 s |
| RAG platform + gateway | 132 | 132 | 0 | 611 ms | 800 ms | 840 ms | 27 ms | 35 ms | 38 ms | 22.77 s | 23.07 s |

## What the model received

| Platform | Chunks in the collection | Model calls | Prompt tokens p50 | p95 | max | Embedding calls | Model's own first token (p50 prompt) | Platform's share of first token p50 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| eneo v2.3.1 | 198 | 180 | 20,369 | 21,941 | 23,505 | 180 | 1622 ms | 1152 ms |
| RAG platform + gateway | 248 | 192 | 3,016 | 4,810 | 4,827 | 38 | 581 ms | 30 ms |

The fake LLM waits 400 ms plus 60 ms per 1 000 prompt tokens before the first token, as a real model reads its prompt; the platform's share is the measured first token minus that.
