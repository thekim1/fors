# Knowledge load test: eneo vs. the RAG platform + gateway

100 staff chatting back to back (no time to read), 10 s ramp, then 90 s measured; 30 % follow-ups in the same conversation. Requests started in the hold are measured to the end of their stream. Fake LLM as glm-5.3-flash (see README.md); the platform under test on 4 vCPUs and 18 GB.
